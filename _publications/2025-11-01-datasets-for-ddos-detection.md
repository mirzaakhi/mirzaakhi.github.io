This paper presents datasets for DDoS attack detection in 5G-enabled Healthcare-IoT (H-IoT) environments. The datasets include realistic network traffic patterns generated from H-IoT devices such as body temperature, heart rate, and oxygen sensors using the Cooja and NS-3 simulators. They comprise labelled traffic samples representing both normal and malicious behaviour. The datasets facilitate the development and evaluation of machine learning-based and deep learning-based cybersecurity methods for H-IoT research and broader IoT intrusion detection applications.
