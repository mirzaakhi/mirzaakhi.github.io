This paper presents a lightweight Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN)-based method for edge deployment on Raspberry Pi 4 devices. The TCN-based approach leverages Healthcare-IoT (H-IoT) DDoS datasets and TensorFlow Lite (TFLite) optimization to enable efficient low-power attack detection in IoT environments. The deployment achieves high detection accuracy with low inference latency and power consumption on MQTT- and UDP-based datasets. The results demonstrate the feasibility of resource-efficient DDoS detection for IoT, IIoT, and H-IoT edge systems.
