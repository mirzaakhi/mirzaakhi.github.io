In this episode of **PhD pULse**, I discuss my academic journey from Bangladesh to the University of Limerick, how my interest in cybersecurity developed through my master’s research, and my PhD work on securing Healthcare IoT systems using artificial intelligence and deep learning.

The conversation also covers cyberattack simulation and dataset generation, the challenges of undertaking a PhD as an international researcher, the importance of mentorship and perseverance, and advice for aspiring researchers.
